# Session flow

The limits and rules in `SKILL.md` apply to every step.

## 1. Intake

One message, at most 3 short questions, skipping what the learner already said: goal and deadline; what they already know (ask for one or two concrete examples, not a self-rating); depth and time per session. If they named source material, base explanations on it, cite sections, and mark outside knowledge. Do not teach in this turn.

## 2. Scope contract and map

One message, at most 8 lines:
- Goal in one line, in the learner's words.
- Depth in one line, as "what a good practitioner can say or do in 2 minutes".
- Map: one line per block, 6 words or fewer, phrased as what the learner will be able to say, ordered by dependency first and payoff second. Do not default to a number of blocks: group the ideas the goal needs into clusters that each fit one 300-word explanation and a 2-question check, split a cluster that needs more, merge tiny ones (2 to 8 is the working range).
- Out of scope: 2 or 3 tempting items.
- End with "Go with this, or adjust?" and wait. If a block later turns out too big, split it and show the updated map in one line.

## 3. Block loop

1. Pretest: one question that makes the learner guess the key idea or quantity. A wrong guess or "I don't know" is fine: react in one sentence, then explain, no hints. Open with a warm-up question on the shaky list when it has items.
2. Explanation: one message covering the whole block (2 or 3 ideas, one concrete example, linked to something the learner knows), with a diagram if the block is a process, structure or comparison, and ending with the `New in this block:` line from `SKILL.md`. If the check will ask the learner to produce something, the explanation contains a worked example of that output with the way each number is derived. Novices get the worked-example sequence from `teaching-moves.md`.
3. Check, which is the gate: two questions, one at a time, closed-book, no hints, using only terms from the ledger lines. The first production attempt in a block is a gap task, the second may be the whole task. From the second block one of them comes from an earlier block, preferably a shaky item. Each has one key point.
4. Feedback per `teaching-moves.md`, then the next block.

Gate outcomes:
- Pass: both correct without hints. Offer cards in one line ("Make cards for this block (up to 6)?"); no clear yes, no cards.
- Fail: re-teach the failed point in up to 100 words, ask a twin question (correct without a hint closes the item and the block passes), carry the item into the next warm-up.
- "Next" while a gate question is open: the gate is not finished. Park the item in one line; the block counts as passed except for that item only if the other question was correct.
- The learner skips the gate: allow it, mark the block unverified, make no cards from it.
- A wrong pretest guess is not shaky by itself, only if the idea is missed again in the gate.
- An unanswered check question returns in the next warm-up, unless the learner said "next".
- A tangent: two sentences, park it, return.

## 4. Wrap-up (up to 150 words)

- Three lines: solid, shaky, next step. Shaky means the learner's concrete gaps from this session, each named, never generic advice.
- A restart note the learner can paste, for example: "Topic X, blocks 1-2 passed, shaky: A and B, next is block 3." On resume skip intake and open with a two-question warm-up on the shaky items.
- Review: with no cards, suggest a self-quiz after 1 day, 1 week and 1 month; with cards, the card app schedules it.
- If cards were not offered yet and a block passed, offer cards and a study note together ("Make cards, a study note, both, or neither?"). A yes already given for per-block cards is not asked again. No clear yes, write nothing.
